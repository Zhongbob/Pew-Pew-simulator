Game Rules

General Rules
  • Each player must register before joining a game.
  • All actions are tracked and scored automatically.
  • Respect other players and follow fair play guidelines.
Scoring System
  • Points are awarded for accuracy and speed.
  • Bonus points for consecutive hits.
  • Penalties for missed shots or rule violations.
Progression
  • Levels unlock as you improve your score.
  • Track your progress on your dashboard.
