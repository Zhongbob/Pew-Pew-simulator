Game Rules

Getting Started
  • There are two gamemodes, freeplay and survival
  • Freeplay involves players shooting targets that appear on the screen.
  • Survival involves players shooting enemies that appear on the screen before they approach the player.
  • Players need at least one computer and a phone to play the game. Create/Join a room in the top right to start.
Calibration
  • Each mobile client acts as a gun.
  • A calibration screen will appear for either gamemode.
  • Shoot at the crosshair to calibrate your device.
Gameplay
  • The mobile device acts as a gun controller.
  • Each Magazine contains 30 rounds. After 30 rounds, the gun will need to be reloaded.
  • To fire a shot, the player needs to disengage the safety, load a magazine, and cock the weapon
  • Firing the weapon has a chance for it to jam, and the player will need to perform the corresponding Immediate Action (IA) drills to remedy it
Gameplay (Freeplay)
  • There are 3 difficulties in freeplay. Choose the one you are comfortable with
  • There is also a night mode in freeplay which will make shooting marginally more difficult.
  • See you accuracy at the bottom right corner of your screen
Gameplay (Survival)
  • In survival mode, players must work together to fend off waves of enemies.
  • Eliminating 20 normal enemies will trigger a boss wave
  • Eliminating a boss will increase the frequency of enemy spawns
